# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 — pre-release 2026-10-25

First packaged PC VR alpha candidate for original Steam Call of Pripyat 1.6.02, based on the pinned OpenXRay engine. Installation and launch confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-25; individual feature regression remains incomplete.

- Stereo rendering, headset/controller tracking, tracked hand poses, support aim, body equipment zones and controller UI.
- Physical firearm reload prototypes, internal/single-round loading, grenade-launcher handling, physical knife and throwable items.
- Stereo binocular/scope rendering, wrist map/status, physical crouching and headset-oriented sound.
- Separate per-user setup, Steam library discovery, desktop shortcut, uninstall with data preservation, bilingual EXE launcher and offline guides.
- Graphics snapshot from the current development config; VR compatibility restrictions preserved. All test kits and unlimited-weight settings disabled in release resources.
- Zone visibility toggle and near-plane depth-precision correction included, still requiring headset confirmation.

See the EN/UK feature-status tables for historical confirmations, unverified latest changes, prototype limits and known regressions. Do not infer complete campaign/hardware support from this list.
