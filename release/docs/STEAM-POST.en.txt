Call of Pripyat VR — free alpha for Quest 2 / Touch (original Steam version)

POST
[h1]Explore the Zone in PC VR[/h1]
I've been working on an unofficial VR mod for the original Steam version of S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Call of Pripyat 1.6.02, built on OpenXRay.

The alpha includes stereoscopic rendering and headset tracking, tracked Touch hands, weapon handling in either hand, two-hand support, physical reloads, stereo binoculars and scopes, grabbing/throwing items, body-based equipment zones, menu rays and physical crouching. It includes its own installer and EXE launcher, plus detailed English and Ukrainian control guides.

[b]Requirements:[/b] the purchased original Steam game (not Enhanced Edition), a Windows x64 PC, and a PC VR connection. Development testing used Meta Quest 2, Touch controllers and USB Quest Link. This is not a standalone Quest APK. Other headsets/runtimes and full campaign completion have not been verified.

[b]This is an alpha, not a finished VR conversion.[/b] Some interactions and weapon variants still need testing. Discarded magazines currently use a shared ammunition pool rather than retaining individual ammunition. Independent dual-firearm use is not supported. The release notes distinguish features previously confirmed in-headset from newer, unverified changes and list known stability issues.

The mod installs separately and references the original game's resources. It does not replace your Steam game files. Please back up saves and start with a new game for testing.

[b]Download and instructions:[/b]
https://github.com/Kerpi4i/call-of-pripyat-vr/releases/tag/v0.1.0-alpha.1

Bug reports with the version, headset/runtime, GPU and clear reproduction steps are welcome. Please read the controls first, especially body slots and manual reloading.
