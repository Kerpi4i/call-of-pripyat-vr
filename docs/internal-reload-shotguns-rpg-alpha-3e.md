# Single-round shotguns and RPG reload prototype, alpha 3e

## Controls and behaviour
Use the existing rear waist grip + index-trigger chord with the free hand to obtain one loading item. For the five new profiles this is a native single 12g round or the original RPG projectile, not a scaled ammunition box. Release grip at the weapon's green loading zone to insert one round. Release elsewhere returns the unused proxy without consuming inventory ammunition. Loose world ammo and ammo extracted from inventory remain distinct from these reload proxies.

### RPG-7
The port is placed 2 cm in front of the tube mouth, local (0, .054, .216); it follows the body bone's actual pose. The native wpn_rpg7_grenade visual is rendered at scale 1, 0.706 m long. Releasing at the port consumes exactly one OG-7B, uses the stock rocket ownership/spawn path, and plays only the final 40 percent of anm_reload plus a native 1.36-2.275 second sound excerpt. It does not wait for manual cocking; firing is available immediately once the owned rocket entity has arrived. FireStart guards that short asynchronous ownership interval so ammunition is not consumed without a projectile. Cosmetic completion uses an idle callback, not eReload, to avoid loading twice. VR weapon-camera animation effects remain disabled.

### Winchester 1300 and SPAS-12
Native capacities remain 7 and 8, including any native upgrade changes to capacity. Every accepted proxy loads exactly one round, with a short insertion sound and no full reload animation or pending lock. Topping up preserves the round already chambered; a newly inserted reserve round does not replace it. Firing remains interruptible while topping up a prepared gun. Loading an empty gun requires an initial pump cycle.

Every shot marks a spent hull and blocks the next shot until the foregrip is pulled fully rearward (90 percent threshold) and returned forward (8 percent threshold). Winchester moves its native zatvor foregrip bone through 10 cm. SPAS moves joint1 through 9 cm and the actual bolt through a linked shorter displacement. Native automatic feed-cartridge visuals and automatic hull emission are suppressed for these two profiles. The spent hull ejects at the rear stop; the next shot becomes available at the forward stop. Releasing halfway leaves the mechanism there and it can be re-grabbed. Short strokes do not prepare the next shot. A direct native bolt grip remains an alternate gesture; the requested foregrip route uses the complete back/forward motion. SPAS is deliberately manual after every shot as requested, despite its stock firing mechanism.

### BM-16 and TOZ-34
Grip the fore-end and lower it relative to the receiver to hinge the barrels, up to 0.65 rad. The gesture uses the actual hinge and snapped foregrip lever rather than a fixed generic arm length. At sufficient opening the spent hulls eject; live rounds stay in their chambers. Each exposed chamber accepts one compatible round. Live/spent masks and individual ammo types determine which round remains and which shoot clip plays, including a single round loaded in either chamber. Projectile origins retain the stock weapon fire-point definitions.

Close by lifting the fore-end back into line, or release it and flick the primary hand upward. The flick uses raw tracking-space hand motion (not player jump/locomotion): upward speed over .8 m/s or upward angular speed over 3 rad/s, after a 250 ms release grace period and with the free hand unoccupied. Flick closure animates at 3 rad/s. Closing plays the last 10 percent of the stock reload motion and its closing/cocking sound; firing waits for that finishing segment. Barrel and cartridge poses are controlled directly while native hammer/lock animations can finish.

## Tracking and rendering
While holding the fore-end of these four articulated shotguns, receiver aim follows the primary Touch with its acquired orientation offset. The support hand articulates the mechanism instead of rotating the entire receiver with the stroke. Authored neutral support contacts are cached to prevent a stock shoot/pump animation from moving the VR hand automatically. Moving support acquisition zones and hand poses follow the actual part. The magazine-fed weapon route is unchanged; an articulation override is keyed to the current internal-loading weapon only.

Casings are temporary native-shell visual objects with gravity and static-world ray contact, no actor collision or inventory entry; lifetime 15 seconds, sharing the existing 24 discarded visual slots. Live-round ejection by deliberately cycling an unfired tube chamber is not simulated yet. The single-round shape is extracted from the original TOZ cartridge mesh, retaining its native material/UVs. No new texture artwork is introduced.

## State and compatibility
Ammo accounting uses native CWeaponAmmo::Get and the real cartridge vectors. The native RPG launcher still creates and owns its projectile. New optional RVI1 state follows the previous optional RVM1 save data: pump/hinge positions, rear latch, chamber live/spent masks and chamber ammo types. Older saves without RVI1 remain readable. Timed cosmetic completion and flick histories reset on load; the physical mechanism state survives. Native full-reload requests cannot bypass the five physical loading profiles. Protecta and RG-6 remain on their previous mechanism pending a separate task.

## Validation
Internal state transition tests cover short-stroke rejection, rear/front cycling, empty first load, interruptible top-up, live versus spent barrel masks, duplicate chamber rejection and single-barrel loading. Existing detachable-magazine tests still pass. Offline validation checks 5 native loading profiles/bones/ports, the single-round mesh and full-size projectile; existing 26 magazine/10 pistol-split/33 handle checks pass. All 54 audio excerpts decode and retain correct mono rate, duration, CRC and native X-Ray metadata. xrGame Release x64 build, engine diff and reverse patch checks are recorded with the checkpoint. No game launch. Gesture directions/ranges, shell appearances and the precise RPG animation seek point require headset verification.

Use copvr_all_weapons_test for the first headset check; it already includes these weapons and reserve ammunition. No additional save was overwritten or game launched during implementation.
