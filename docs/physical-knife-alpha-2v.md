# Tracked physical knife combat, alpha 2v

VR local actor knife FireStart, Fire2Start and trigger Action paths no longer start native attacks. KnifeStrike also rejects legacy VR strikes, preventing motion-mark attacks from producing the original forward damage cone. Desktop/AI knife behavior is preserved. Knife draw/hide resets tracked swing state.

After the tracked HUD item pose is solved, update physical melee using that actual knife pose and its animated knife bone (current transform times inverse bind). Measured native blade endpoints: heel (0,0.004,0.030) and tip (0.000083,0.004791,0.170585) metres. Trace the swept blade at seven points with three narrow edge offsets plus its current length, against both world and dynamic collision. No native metre-long attack ray, splash victim search or button-driven animation is used in VR.

Speed is measured in the player tracking base, removing character locomotion/turning from the damage estimate. Use the faster endpoint of the rigid blade. Only continuous samples with <=100 ms interval, <=12 m/s tracking speed and <=45 cm world tip displacement are accepted. Repeated timestamps, tracking loss, invalid HUD pose, hand changes, menu/dialogue/pause, hiding and equipment transitions disarm/reset the sweep. Both primary hands are supported.

A strike requires >=0.8 m/s after a short rest/arming period. Damage scales as speed/3, capped at 2: 3 m/s yields native primary knife damage, 6 m/s yields twice native primary damage. Native difficulty-based knife power, wound type and impulse feed a normal GE_HIT event with actual target bone/local hit position and actor/weapon attribution. Impact impulse follows measured blade motion. Contact plays native knife sound and Touch haptics. Static surfaces consume a strike as well. Check visibility from the handle to the contact before damaging a target behind geometry.

One impact per uninterrupted swing. A new stroke requires at least 180 ms plus 80 ms rest or a clear reversal; holding/moving a knife continuously inside a target does not apply frame-by-frame damage. Very fast tracking jumps disarm instead of dealing damage.

Validation: knife melee tests cover minimum speed, increasing/capped damage, equip arming, continuous-swing suppression, reverse stroke, cooldown/rest and tracking-jump reset. First xrGame build passed; final build includes transition guards and impact direction correction. No game launch or empirical NPC/crate/contact verification yet. Ray sweeps approximate the blade's collision volume; this is not a rigid-body weapon simulation. Native bullet-manager decals/material impact particles are not generated by this direct contact event.
